What is measured

These are received and sent traffic across WDI operations. The IP figure counts different public addresses observed across those operations; countries are counted from the locations attached to those addresses.

Short ranges sum current app feeds. The longer ranges combine container traffic and newer application measurements without counting the same traffic twice. The 30-day view sums observed minutes into hourly buckets; partial hours remain recorded totals, without extrapolation. Missing buckets stay gaps. Late readings can revise recent buckets.

Source and timing

— recently reporting runtime identities. Units are decimal; timestamps are UTC. History does not expose complete observation durations, so recorded MB/minute is not duration-normalized throughput.

What does WDI provide?+

WDI sources and operates residential node capacity for public-web data networks. The work covers provider discussions, permitted workloads, deployment and operational measurement. Data collection and downstream AI use sit with the participating networks and buyers.

What capacity fits?+

Distinct residential connections, compatible devices or compute, and permission for the agreed workload. Geography, bandwidth, availability and provider limits are assessed before a pilot. The supplier guide explains what to share.

How do Grass and Wynd Labs fit?+

Grass is the primary network for WDI’s node participation, with Wynd Labs on the data-demand side. WDI supplies and operates the residential access layer; downstream uses include AI pre-training, multimodal data and live context retrieval.

Do the IP figures show available capacity?+

The IP figure counts different public addresses observed across WDI operations. Current availability is assessed separately for a specific requirement. The measurement guide explains the distinction.

How are scope and commercial terms agreed?+

Start with the workload, connection requirements, regions, resources and expected duration. Pilot scope, pricing and operating responsibilities are confirmed in a separate agreement before a deployment begins.

How can I raise an operational concern?+

Email contact@webdatainfrastructure.com with the relevant service, time and a description. Please keep passwords, keys and other credentials out of the message.
