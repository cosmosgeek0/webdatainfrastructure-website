Privacy policy

How information is handled when you visit or contact WDI.

Updated 26 September 2026

Scope and contact

This notice covers the public Web Data Infrastructure website and enquiries sent through it. WDI is operated by CosmosGeek. Questions about personal information can be sent to contact@webdatainfrastructure.com.

Information you provide

The enquiry form sends your name, email address and enquiry type, together with any organization, message or proposed meeting details you choose to provide. We use this information to respond, assess the enquiry and arrange a conversation. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Services that process information

FormSubmit relays form submissions to WDI’s email inbox. Our hosting and email services process information needed to deliver the website and correspondence. Cloudflare provides website delivery, security and performance services; technical information can include IP addresses, browser and device information, requested pages and request timing.

See FormSubmit’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for their processing practices. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Browser storage

The website uses local storage to remember that you have seen the opening brand animation, and session storage to remember when you dismiss the enquiry form. The opening preference stays in your browser until its website storage is cleared; the enquiry preference lasts for your browsing session. Infrastructure providers may use their own security or operational storage. You can control cookies and website storage through your browser settings.

Operating measurements

The public traffic display uses aggregate measurements from WDI operations. It publishes aggregate traffic and recorded country coverage, without raw IP lists, account identifiers or private control interfaces. Website visitors are not enrolled as network nodes by viewing these measurements.

Correspondence and your requests

Enquiries become part of WDI’s email correspondence. To ask about information you provided, or request access, correction or deletion, email contact@webdatainfrastructure.com. Some correspondence may need to be retained for an ongoing business matter or applicable recordkeeping obligations. Third-party service retention is governed by those services’ policies.

Updates

Changes to this notice will appear on this page with an updated date. External websites linked from WDI have their own privacy practices.
